# Probe-to-Gene Mapping

## Required decisions

- Annotation source and version.
- Gene-symbol namespace.
- Treatment of unmapped probes.
- Treatment of probes mapping to multiple genes.
- Representative-probe rule for gene-labelled figures.
- Gene-expression collapse rule for pathway analyses.
- Whether probe-level statistics remain the primary genome-wide output.

## Recommended outputs

- cleaned platform annotation;
- ambiguous mappings table;
- unmapped probes table;
- representative-probe table;
- gene-level expression matrix.

Different mapping strategies must not be presented as interchangeable.
