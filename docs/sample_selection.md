# Sample Selection

Document the exact rules used to select the 24 asthmatic samples.

Include:

- all candidate GEO accessions;
- inclusion and exclusion criteria;
- ancestry-label source fields;
- sex/gender-label source fields;
- tissue and disease-status confirmation;
- exclusions and reasons;
- final group counts;
- checks for duplicated or mismatched sample IDs.

Do not assign ancestry solely by sample order without a documented metadata mapping.
