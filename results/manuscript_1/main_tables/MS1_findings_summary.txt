INTERPRETATION AND LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Whole blood is a cell mixture. Ancestry-associated differences in leucocyte
  composition, particularly the Duffy-null neutrophil phenotype, are a plausible
  driver of part of this signal. Deconvolution is not performed here, so
  cell-composition and cell-intrinsic effects cannot be separated.

  With 12 samples per ancestry group the study is underpowered for small effects.
  Subgroup co-expression networks use 8 to 12 samples and are exploratory only.

  Quality control identified no outlier samples. It does not establish the absence
  of batch effects; no batch covariate is available in the GEO metadata.

  Findings are associations within a single severe-asthma cohort and require
  replication in an independent, ancestry-diverse dataset before any clinical
  or mechanistic claim is made.
